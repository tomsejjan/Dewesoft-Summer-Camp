# Strain Gauge Challenge

## Objective

The objective of this challenge was to gain hands-on experience with
strain-gauge installation, calibration and measurement.

## Strain Gauge Installation

The strain gauge was applied to the measured structure and prepared for
connection to the Dewesoft measurement system.

Important aspects included:
- preparation of the measurement surface,
- correct strain-gauge orientation,
- reliable mechanical bonding, etc.

## Measurement Setup

The strain-gauge signal was connected to the Dewesoft data-acquisition
system and configured in DewesoftX.

Before performing the measurement, the channel had to be correctly
configured and calibrated.

This included checking:
- sensor configuration,
- bridge setup - full bridge/half bridge,
- zero balance.
  
## Result

The challenge provided practical experience with strain-gauge
installation and calibration using Dewesoft
hardware and DewesoftX.

Detailed measurement results will be added later.
