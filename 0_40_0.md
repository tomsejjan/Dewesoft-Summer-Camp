# 0_40_0 Challenge
The objective of the challenge was to measure the complete 0–40–0 km/h maneuver of the Mini Cooper.

The vehicle had to accelerate from standstill to 40 km/h and subsequently brake back to 0 km/h, while the complete maneuver was recorded as a single measurement.

The challenge was inspired by the 0–400–0 performance tests of the Rimac Nevera, scaled down to the possibilities of the Mini Cooper used during the Summer Camp.

## Data Evaluation

The recorded signals provided different views of the same vehicle motion.

Acceleration directly showed the longitudinal forces acting during acceleration and braking, while the velocity signal provided a convenient way to identify the 40 km/h target and the final stop.

The synchronized measurement made it possible to compare these quantities on a common time base and evaluate the complete maneuver objectively.
