# CAN Analysis

## Objective

Analysis of vehicle CAN-bus data during the Dewesoft Summer Camp vehicle measurement challenges.

## Measurement Setup

CAN data from the Mini Cooper were acquired together with other measurement
channels in DewesoftX.

## Measurement

This challenge focused on working with vehicle CAN signals and comparing
them with data acquired by the external measurement system.

## Result

Documentation and detailed results are currently being completed.
